Experiment: no-learning-during-throttle

Observations:

- After warm-up, pressure stabilizes in a narrow band (~1.2–1.55).
- Throttling is predominantly LIGHT, with occasional MEDIUM spikes.
- Once throttling begins, baseline updates stop, causing the system to freeze its learned state.

- Major faults quickly drop to ~0, while minor faults dominate the signal.
- Despite large fluctuations in minor faults, pressure remains relatively stable.

- When workload intensity drops significantly (minor faults fall to near zero), pressure does not immediately reflect this change due to frozen baseline.

- System exhibits low responsiveness to dynamic workload changes, especially after initial throttling phase.

Conclusion:
Skipping baseline updates during throttling yields a stable but under-adaptive controller, leading to conservative throttling and reduced sensitivity to workload variation.
